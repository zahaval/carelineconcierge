    Let us handlethe communication,while you earn the trust.

    CareLine Concierge is a 24/7 resident and family concierge service designed specifically for nursing homes, providing dedicated support, communication, and assistance from admission through discharge.

    We provide residents and their families with a dedicated point of contact they can call or text for any need, from everyday requests to more complex or time-sensitive matters. We ensure each concern is heard, properly documented, communicated to the appropriate team, and followed through until it is addressed.

    But CareLine Concierge is more than a communication service. Every call and text becomes valuable insight for the facility. Our platform gives nursing home owners, administrators, and leadership real-time visibility into what residents and families are experiencing, helping identify patterns, improve accountability, and strengthen the resident experience.

    CareLine Concierge also supports the admission and discharge process, gathers resident and family feedback, and provides facilities with organized data and reporting they can use to make better operational decisions.

    Just like care doesn't stop after hours, neither should communication.
